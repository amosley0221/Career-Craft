9. Risks, Guardrails, and Decision Rules

This section helps you make a clear decision instead of drifting.

Decision Rules

Choose UNC Charlotte first if: transfer fit is reasonable, you meet GPA/math requirements, online course sequencing works, and you are willing to build coding strength consistently.

Choose ECU MIS if: the business-tech route feels more motivating, the transfer path is much cleaner, or the coding-heavy path looks unlikely to be sustainable.

Keep UNC Charlotte B.S. AI as watchlist if: you confirm online flexibility, math requirements, and career fit, and you decide you want a deeper AI/ML route rather than an applied software route.
