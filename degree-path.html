6. Evidence Behind the Scores

Reasoning from the original Version 1 board. Use the source tracker below to re-check facts over time.

Career upside

The UNC Charlotte CS path has the stronger technical labor-market match. BLS reports a May 2024 median annual wage of $133,080 for software developers, with software developer employment projected to grow 16% from 2024 to 2034. Data science is also strong, with BLS reporting a May 2024 median wage of $112,590 and projected growth of 34% from 2024 to 2034.

ECU MIS still has strong career relevance. BLS reports computer systems analysts had a May 2024 median wage of $103,790 and projected growth of 9% from 2024 to 2034. MIS can also lead toward IT management over time; BLS reports computer and information systems managers had a May 2024 median wage of $171,200, though that role typically requires experience beyond the bachelor's degree.

Transfer/admission practicality

UNC Charlotte's online B.A. CS program requires transfer students to have 24+ transferable credit hours, a 2.5+ cumulative GPA, a C or higher in previously attempted CS courses, and a C or better in college-level algebra equivalent to MATH 1100.

ECU's MIS transfer guide says transfer applicants need 24 transferable hours and a 2.0 cumulative GPA to apply, but to declare MIS as the major, students need 45 semester hours, a 2.5 overall GPA, and completion of eight declaration courses with C- or higher. ECU also notes there is no transfer credit limit, though at least 25% of degree hours and at least 50% of major hours must be completed through ECU enrollment.

Online flexibility

UNC Charlotte describes the AI-Assisted Software Engineering B.A. as 100% online and asynchronous, which is excellent for a working/adult student schedule.

ECU says students can complete the BSBA online in several concentrations, including Management Information Systems, and that the online and face-to-face degree are the same.

Cost/value

UNC Charlotte lists the online B.A. CS tuition at $189.48 per credit hour for NC residents and $864.73 per credit hour for non-residents, with rates subject to change.

ECU's Fall 2026 distance education resident undergraduate table lists a new Fall 2026 resident cohort at $213.42 for one credit hour and $2,555.54 for 12+ credit hours, with undergraduate billing capped at 12 credit hours and pricing subject to change.

Honest read

Right now, treat UNC Charlotte AI-Assisted Software Engineering as your highest-upside target. It gives you the clearest tech-builder identity: software, AI tools, databases, front end, back end, architecture, capstone, and portfolio potential.

Treat ECU MIS as your safer tech-business alternate. It is not a weak option. It may actually be the better choice if you decide you want technology, systems, business analysis, project management, and management more than daily coding.

The biggest unresolved question is not "which school is better?" It is: Do you want to become the person who builds software, or the person who manages, analyzes, and improves business technology systems?
